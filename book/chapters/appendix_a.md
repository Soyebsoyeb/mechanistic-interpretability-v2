# Appendix A — Linear Algebra

## Matrix Multiplication
For $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ and $B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k}$, $AB \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times k}$.

## Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues
$Av = \lambda v$ where $v$ is eigenvector, $\lambda$ is eigenvalue.

## Orthogonality
$x^\top y = 0$ implies $x$ and $y$ are orthogonal.

## Projection
For orthonormal basis $U$: $P = UU^\top$.

## SVD
$W = U\Sigma V^\top$ where $U, V$ orthogonal, $\Sigma$ diagonal.

## Trace and Frobenius Norm
$\text{tr}(A) = \sum_i A_{ii}$, $\|A\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i,j} A_{ij}^2}$.

## Positive Semi-Definite Matrices
$A \succeq 0$ iff $x^\top A x \geq 0$ for all $x$.
