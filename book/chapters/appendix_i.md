# Appendix I — How to Read a Research Paper

## For Every Paper, Extract

### Claim
What does the paper say? State the main claim in one sentence.

### Evidence
What experiment supports it? Describe the key experiment in detail.

### Mechanism
What internal process is proposed? What is the claimed algorithm or circuit?

### Assumptions
What must be true for the interpretation to hold? List unstated assumptions.

### Alternative Explanations
What else could explain the result? Be creative and critical.

### Reproduction
Can the experiment be reproduced? What information is missing?

### Extension
What experiment would meaningfully test the claim further?

## Questions to Ask
1. Is the claim causal or correlational?
2. What is the effect size?
3. How many examples were tested?
4. Were negative results reported?
5. Does the mechanism generalize?
6. What would falsify the claim?
