# Appendix E — Causal Inference

- Structural equations: $X_i = f_i(Pa_i, U_i)$
- Intervention: $do(X=x)$ replaces structural equation
- $P(Y|X=x) \neq P(Y|do(X=x))$
- Back-door criterion: control for confounders
- Front-door criterion: indirect identification
- Potential outcomes: $Y_0, Y_1$ for treatment/control
