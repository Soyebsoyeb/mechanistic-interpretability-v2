# Appendix I — How to Read a Research Paper

For every paper, extract:

- **Claim**: What does the paper say?
- **Evidence**: What experiment supports it?
- **Mechanism**: What internal process is proposed?
- **Assumptions**: What must be true for the interpretation to hold?
- **Alternative explanations**: What else could explain the result?
- **Reproduction**: Can the experiment be reproduced?
- **Extension**: What experiment would meaningfully test the claim further?

## Red Flags
- Claims based solely on visualization
- No causal intervention
- No held-out testing
- No discussion of limitations
- Cherry-picked examples only
